Parser policy: bounded sfnt container/table/checksum/OS2/outline preflight before

face0, regular400/width5/upright only. Collections, variable/color/bitmap/SVG,
unknown/restricted embedding and unsupported styles refuse. This is not a full
OpenType sanitizer or an OS process sandbox. OS/2 embedding rules are based on
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/opentype/spec/os2#fstype;
container rules: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/opentype/spec/otff.
Local license permission is an explicit caller review record, not legal advice
inferred from fsType. Later writer validation must retain this record.
