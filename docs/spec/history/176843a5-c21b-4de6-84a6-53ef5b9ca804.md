## Severity-ranked required fixes

### F01 — P1: Same-slot replacement does not yet prove unchanged canvas pixels

Owner: Architecture author, with UX author mirroring behavior. Exact sources: ARCH-1 §§3.1, 3.2, 3.4 and S1; UX-1.1 §3 “Result placement and history”, J10/J12 and §12.

Failing criteria: Task 4 criterion 2, especially “alpha/color/resampling … output placement and original-content preservation”; Task 3 criterion 5, especially “transparency/color policy and non-destructive acceptance” and joint raster reconciliation. Also the accepted preservation policy / REQ-UX / REQ-STATE.

Evidence: §3.1 specifies linear-light premultiplied arithmetic and quantization. §3.2 captures transformed/opacity/masked contribution into RGBA8. §3.4 inserts that captured contribution at opacity 1 and claims unchanged current-document exterior. No rule requires the ordinary document compositor to materialize each layer contribution into that same quantized RGBA8 form before blending the original stack.

Counterexample at a pixel where M=0: opaque white native layer, opacity 0.1, over opaque black. Direct linear stack composite quantizes to sRGB byte 89. Baking opacity into the source snapshot quantizes alpha to 26/255; reinserting white/alpha26 at opacity 1 over black quantizes to byte 90. Prepared=S can hold byte-for-byte while the visible canonical document changes. Hiding the original avoids double-composition but does not solve this precision change.

Minimal required change: specify one normative contribution pipeline shared by ordinary document composition/export, capture, comparison and replacement. A bounded solution is to materialize the same versioned RGBA8 contribution after transform/layer-mask/opacity, consume those exact bytes in the ordinary stack, and reuse them without reapplying effects or a second lossy conversion at identity placement. If another precision model is selected, prove the same exterior invariant and reconcile its formats/memory consequences. Do not merely weaken the accepted preservation promise or say “test later”.

Re-verify: run Check R1 below, then show before/after equality under the corrected normative model for opacity 0.1, noninteger mask coverage, translucent backgrounds, transformed/filter-edge pixels, zero-alpha hidden RGB, partial request crops and merged-visible replacement. Verify original stack slot, opacity 1, no reapplication of source mask, visibility preconditions and exact undo. Add the concrete counterexample to S1 and UX acceptance outcomes.

Author clarification: Architecture author explicitly confirmed on 2026-09-25 at 20:32 UTC that no existing clause supplies the missing materialization stage and the stronger exterior claim is overstrong. No source edits were made during review.

### F02 — P1: Feather support can leave the approved request crop without a defined resolution

Owner: Architecture author for the rule; UX author for preview, blocking error and resolution. Exact sources: ARCH-1 §3 opening preconditions, §§3.2–3.3 and S1; UX-1.1 §3 source/mask table, J10 and §12.

Failing criteria: Task 4 criterion 2 (crop/padding, mask alignment/feathering and placement); Task 3 criterion 5 (mask feathering/source and result semantics).

Evidence: documentGridSource is the full document; a crop-local mask is translated there. Finite-support blur expands nonzero effective M. The provider receives only the approved crop. §3.3 both interpolates wherever M is nonzero and copies S outside request crop/padding support. There is no rule expanding or clipping/reapproving the domain before freezing.

Counterexample: 12-pixel-wide document, crop x in [4,8), hard mask fully covering that crop. A radius-2 sampled triangle with normalized weights [0,1/4,1/2,1/4,0] gives M(3)=M(8)=16384 outside the request. This persists for ordinary nonzero feather kernels regardless of the later sealed rounding convention. Provider G has no samples for that extra approved edit region. Silent copy clips the advertised feather; extrapolating G or expanding the crop changes another part of the contract. “Include reconstruction halo” in binary-mask generation cannot create provider pixels outside its finite request rectangle.

Minimal required change: before request approval/acceptance, resolve effective support and reconstruction needs against the approved request domain. Block an insufficient domain and offer explicit previewed crop/padding expansion plus renewed source/size approval, or explicit feather clipping/repreview that finalizes a new M with zero outside the approved domain. Freeze resolved M, source, domain, transform and approval together. Define document-edge boundary handling separately. Never silently clip an already approved mask or resize the request.

Re-verify: Check R2 plus edge/corner masks, radius 0, full/empty crop masks, fractional bounds, approved downsampling, output-size mismatch and document-edge clipping. J10 must show the exact state, user options and approval invalidation; S1 must show both chosen branches and the no-submit state before resolution.

Author clarification: Architecture author confirmed at 20:32 UTC that “zero outside approved support” and generic crop-approval invalidation do not resolve this conflict. Proposed containment plus explicit expand/clip recovery agrees with the minimal fix; the source remains unchanged.
